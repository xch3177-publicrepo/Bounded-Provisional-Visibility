## What may be written

- The **observable query-path hiding behaviour depended on the read-consistency
  mode**. Never "delta_hide is consistency-dependent" — under Strong and Session
  it was never point-estimated, so the underlying delay may be identical and
  only its visibility differ.
- Bounded/Eventually: provisional items **remained observable across dozens of
  completed probes** before query-path exclusion. Quote the stale-probe medians
  and maxima; they are counted, not inferred.
- Bounded/Eventually: empirical hide-confirmation delay **as a rounded
  magnitude or range** — P50 ~290–330 ms, P95 ~380–430 ms — with the 9–11 ms
  transition envelope. *(Corrected 2026-07-26: this line first said "~420–430
  ms", which is the Strong/Session probe duration, not the range in the four
  cells where the delay was resolved. The JSON is authoritative, including over
  this file.)*
- Strong/Session: `delta_hide` ∈ [0, ~420 ms], **censored inside the first
  completed probe, no point estimate**.
- "No material ANN-path overhead from the scalar probe was observed under the
  evaluated workload."

## What may not be written

- **Any backlog or between-consistency difference as a system effect.**
  *(Corrected 2026-07-26.)* The 2–10 ms figure first written here came from the
  Strong/Session cells, where $\delta_{hide}$ is not a measurement at all. In the
  cells where it **was** resolved the backlog contrast changes sign — Bounded
  P95 380.5→430.2 ms ($+50$), Eventually 412.6→389.9 ms ($-23$) — at the same
  order as the 22.3 ms control drift. So the honest sentence is: *no backlog
  effect was resolved by this run; the contrast is inconsistent in direction and
  of the same order as the environment's own drift.* That is not evidence that
  backlog has no effect, and the earlier "smaller than the control drift"
  phrasing understated the spread.
- **Internal propagation latency.** Never measured, never separable from probe
  execution and cadence. `delta_prop` does not appear in any S2 result.
- **The 419–421 ms figures for Strong/Session as delta_hide.** That is the probe
  duration — the instrument, not the measurement.
- **P99 of anything.** 200 events per cell support a median firmly and a P95 at
  its limit; P99 needs 1000.
- **Pooled statistics across runs.** Runs are independent repetitions; report
  run-level P95 and the range.
